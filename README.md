# ZDNet

ZDNet is a business technology news website owned by Ziff Davis, founded in 1991 as a general interest technology portal. Originally part of Ziff-Davis Publishing, the site has passed through several ownership changes — including CBS Corporation/ViacomCBS and Red Ventures — before returning to Ziff Davis in 2024. ZDNet delivers news, analysis, reviews, and how-to guides covering enterprise IT, cybersecurity, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, hardware, software, and business innovation, serving IT managers, technology decision-makers, and tech-savvy business professionals.

**URL:** [https://www.zdnet.com/](https://www.zdnet.com/)

## APIs

ZDNet does not offer a public developer API. However, ZDNet provides RSS feeds for programmatic access to its news content. The main news feed is available at [https://www.zdnet.com/news/rss.xml](https://www.zdnet.com/news/rss.xml), and topic-specific feeds follow the pattern `https://www.zdnet.com/topic/[topic-name]/rss.xml`, covering areas such as security, cloud, artificial intelligence, and more.
